# EC349 — Two separately pinned channel SHA attestations and signed receipt-time provenance

**Construction V6.59 / R349 / EC349**. This is a bounded, **offline READ-ONLY** cryptographic importer. Native PowerShell remains V4.60 / R150. The term “independent channel” refers to **distinct declared actors and keys in supplied originals**, not an independently verified institution, machine, clock or repository.

## Data authority and exact inherited history

Supply the complete argument set of `EC348.audit_external_custody`, including full EC347→EC337 SHA-pinned original paths, EC348 keeper directory/public root, every keeper signed original (including explicit literal `UNPINNED` where the external SHA pin was not obtained), and all explicit optional keeper-copy retests. EC349 **actually calls** `audit_external_custody` and requires a properly verified `exit_code=2`; a caller-created “already verified” status has no input channel. The `ec348_inputs_canonical_sha256` and `ec348_verified_result_canonical_sha256` bind each new signed document to the exact complete source input and computed source result, not just a chosen “good” keeper.

`derive-handoff` is **unsigned**. Its `keeper_visible_heads` lists every keeper in EC348 roster order, all visible head raw SHA values (including forks), ledger SHA, pinned ancestry and expiry-gap flags, and outcome. `keeper_signed_originals` includes the raw SHA actually read for each EC348 receipt and whether its SHA was **independently supplied**; converting `UNPINNED` to a local digest never makes an external pin.

## Two channel roots and three roles per channel

For `audit-dual-anchors`, exactly **two** channel directory originals must be individually supplied with **their whole-original SHA-256** and a separately provided 32-byte Ed25519 root public key. Channel directories must be in ascending `channel_id` order, with two different IDs. Each directory is an Ed25519-signed document with outer `{schema,claim,signature_ed25519}` and domain `CES/EC349/IndependentChannelDirectory/v1\0`. Its exact claim fields are `DIR_FIELDS` in `anchor.py` and bind case, both complete EC348 digests, keeper directory original SHA, independent root/receipt/time actors, claimed validity window, limited scope and explicit non-authority.

There are **six required, role-distinct actor triples** `{signer_id, public_hex, organization_id}`: each channel has a directory-signing root, a receipt signer and a time witness. No actor ID, public key or claimed organization may overlap with the other five, any EC348 keeper/root, or the prior EC343/EC342 registered signers. The caller supplies trust roots; CES **does not authenticate** legal institutions or outside provenance. The root's private key and every signing private key remain **outside this auditor and the unified console**.

## Versioned out-of-band receipt, whole-original SHA and forks

Each channel can present **0–6** signed receipt originals (total up to **12**) in exact channel/sequence/original-SHA order, at most three sequence numbers and two siblings per sequence. Each document is signed under `CES/EC349/OutOfBandSHAReceipt/v1\0` by that directory's receipt actor. Its exact `RECEIPT_FIELDS` must bind all of: case and complete EC348 SHA context; independently pinned directory SHA; channel and signer identity; **every** EC348 visible keeper head and every furnished signed keeper original with pin presence; `capture_claim` for sequence 1 or `renewal_claim|revocation_claim` for later sequences; SHA and path of an *explicitly provided already verified* predecessor; fresh global nonce; reported receipt and expiry UTC; explicit lack of remote authority.

Signatures, SHA, canonical ordering, same-channel predecessor, monotonic claimed receipt times and no renewal after signed revocation are enforced. All branches remain visible; if multiple heads exist, **no winner or implicit head/time witness** is selected. An unpinned first version remains unpinned after later pinned renewals; a renewal after its previous signed expiry is reported as a signed-claim **time continuity gap**, not repaired. Receipt claim and directory UTC are self-reported and cannot exceed the auditor-provided dual evaluation UTC; that evaluation itself is not a trusted clock.

## Separate receipt-time witness original and cross-channel comparison

A witness original must explicitly bind the **whole SHA of a receipt actually supplied in this audit**, its directory SHA, channel ID, statement sequence, its separate time actor, `claimed_observed_utc`, `uncertainty_seconds` (0–3600), and `separately_signed_local_clock_claim`. Its signature uses a **different Ed25519 domain** `CES/EC349/IndependentReceiptTimeWitness/v1\0`. The exact `TIME_FIELDS` list lives in `anchor.py`. At most one time witness original may be provided for each signed receipt, and **only the unique latest unforked head's witness** can be used for that channel's bounded current time comparison. Every witness whole-original SHA must be given, or explicitly `UNPINNED`; a local digest never upgrades provenance.

If no witness is presented, or its independent whole SHA was not furnished, report that uncertainty. Show the exact signed time gap in seconds, bound it by both the witness's claimed uncertainty and a conservative local comparison cap of 300 seconds. If both channels have unambiguous witnessed heads, report their separate signed timestamps and flag any **more-than-300-second difference**. These calculations compare signed self-reports; they cannot certify real-world elapsed time or independent UTC.

The former needs all EC348 source arguments; the latter additionally needs `--dual-evaluation-utc`, two each `--channel-directory-file`, `--expected-channel-directory-sha256`, `--expected-channel-root-public-hex`, and repeatable aligned `--channel-receipt-file` + `--expected-channel-receipt-sha256`, `--time-witness-file` + `--expected-time-witness-sha256`. It uses **fixed** `python -I -B scripts/ces_cas_dual_channel_anchor.py audit-dual-anchors`, validates paths, bounds, response schema, negative-authority flags and exit code. The unified route **never exposes** signing, private key, copied bytes, network calls, CAS/WORM writers or a provider connection.

Nine per-channel outcomes (`OUTCOMES`) distinguish no original, fork, signed revocation, unpinned ancestry, absent/unpinned time witness, time divergence, claimed expiry/time gap, inherited unresolved EC348 keeper history, and pinned consistent local cryptographic evidence. Counts/status are bounded and every legitimate case returns `exit_code=2`, `*_unresolved`. **Even the last outcome is not proof of two independent outside organizations**, real custody, certified timestamp, no hidden forks, physical long-term durability, remote Provider/CAS/WORM execution or nonexecution.
